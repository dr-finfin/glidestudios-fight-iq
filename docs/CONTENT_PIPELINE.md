# Fight IQ Content Pipeline

## Editorial categories

1. Fighter stories
2. Rivalries
3. Fight stories
4. What happened to / where are they now

## Story lifecycle

```text
idea
→ research
→ script
→ production
→ review
→ approved
→ published
```

## Story scoring

Score out of 80:

- Narrative strength: 10
- Conflict: 10
- Emotional stakes: 10
- Curiosity: 10
- Fighter popularity: 10
- Visual potential: 10
- Search potential: 10
- Evergreen potential: 10

## Quality rule

Research must distinguish between verified facts, observations and analysis. Important factual claims should have supporting sources before publication.
